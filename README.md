# Ashfaq — portfolio

A tailor's-sketchbook portfolio built with Next.js 16 (App Router, Cache Components).

- **Intro** (`app/components/crossroads/`): on a first visit the home page opens on a little story drawn straight onto the site's own desk (the canvas is transparent, so the paper, pencil shading and stray marks are the page's). A road comes up the page and forks into four lanes, unrolled like bolts of cloth off into the distance between low pencil hills, a running stitch down each; a signpost drops in at the fork and its boards swing round on their nails: Tailoring, Distributed systems, Economics, Linux; "Pick a lane." is written where the hero's headline stands. I walk off up the Tailoring lane, stop and shrug; turn round and walk back; an idea (a comic panel: a grin, then a finger up); turn again and take out the shears (a glint, and "snip!" lettered at each snip): two snips cut the lanes loose, their ends curling and threads falling, as "or don't." is scribbled after the headline. The lanes swing together into one striped road, every sign spinning to point up it, a needle laces the stripes into one, and I walk off up it as the view eases in after me. Then a drop of burgundy watercolour falls where the road goes off and blooms out across the paper: under its ragged, spreading edge (darker at the rim, as watercolour dries) the story gives way and the site comes up, and the wash dries away, while the headline holds still; the hero's own headline takes over from the intro's in the same frame. I'm drawn frame by frame: the sheet in `design/crossroads/frames.png` is cut out, keyed off its white ground, its cropped frames' legs let down to the others' proportions and shod, anchored between the feet and packed into `public/intro/crossroads.webp` (with `frames.json`) by `npm run intro:art`; the page fetches it from the head the moment the intro is decided on. The rest is drawn in code, in ink that boils as hand-drawn animation does, with springy motion; the headline is measured off the hero itself, so it gives way to the hero's exactly. About 13 s, paced so each beat can be followed; first visit per session only; skippable at any moment (even before the scripts load); never for deep links or reduced motion.
- **World view** (`/worldview`, `app/components/film/`): *All of us*, my world view as a film of ninety seconds about exploitation and the revolt against it, cut like anime and drawn entirely in code on one canvas, with no pictures. Some forty shots with hard cuts (the shot list is `design/experience_my_world_view/FILM_SCRIPT.md`): a clock, and between its ticks tired eyes; a line of grey workers running off down a cold hall, every pump sending a coin up a pipe to a glass tower with a top hat and a cigar on it; a press, and with every slam the frame splits into more, narrower panels; one worker falls and nobody looks. One stops. His eye opens red, the first colour; he tears a strip from the belt and ties it round his head: **NO.** Then the march, and a cut on every beat (an eye, a fist, a knot, **TWO.**, **TEN.**), the frame doubling into 2, 4, 8, 16 red eyes, **ALL OF US.**, a run at ground level, the roof torn off to a sunset full of flags. The hat rises over the horizon, shields slam down, they charge, and at the blow the picture itself tears in half. He gets up out of the rubble with the flag, the sun breaks, the wall shatters, the gold comes down as light, and it ends at one long table in the morning: *Not a ladder. A table.* The colour tells it: cold blue-grey, then red and a hot sunset, then gold. Every shot is a drawing of a 1600 × 900 stage (painted far beyond its edges, so a phone sees more sky and ground instead of bars) seen through that shot's camera, and all of it is a function of the film's clock (`film.ts` for the frame, `act1.ts` to `act3.ts` for the shots, `sets.ts` for the places and close-ups, `draw.ts` for the kit): so it can be paused, jumped (a mark on the timeline for each of the eight scenes, space, the arrow keys) and replayed, and any frame saved as a poster. The people are figures on a small skeleton, in two flat tones inside an ink outline, with a hair silhouette and a headband whose tails stream in the wind; they move on twos, as drawn animation does, while the camera and the effects (speed lines, focus lines, impact frames, a soft bloom) run smooth. The picture can be played with: in the shot down the line, click a worker and they wake; during the squeeze, press and hold and the press can't come down; anywhere, drag and the picture tears and is sewn back; "Explore" loops the scene you are in. Sound is off until asked for. The music is two recordings and nothing else (Warszawianka for the revolt, cut dead at the tear; the Internationale for the rise), each started silently a moment early so it is in step with the film's clock when it is let through, and the cuts are on the march's beat; in the factory there is only the machine. The effects are made in the browser (`sound.ts`). With reduced motion it is eight still posters with a caption each; without JavaScript, and for a screen reader, the film is there in words.
- **The door** (`app/components/site/WorldDoor.tsx`): the home page ends on a red rag on a stick, the only bright red on a site drawn in one burgundy ink (`--flag`), stirring in the wind; it opens the world view. Site-wide, a link to a section glides there, the scrollbar is thread on paper, and pages that load afresh cross-fade.
- **Hero** (`app/components/hero/`): a four-stage, thread-and-cloth identity animation.
- **Thread Board blog** (`app/components/threadboard/`, `lib/blog/`): articles are pins tied together by threads. Clicking a pin opens a quick read; the full article lives at `/blog/<slug>`.
- **Education** (`app/components/study/`): a plate from an astronomy notebook. Me, as a still illustration on the page (my own colours toned to the paper, inked contours, a few hatched strokes in the folds), with a halo of dust hugging my outline, motes circling me and dust rising off me, all drawn towards whichever subject is pointed at, with my three subjects orbiting as balls of particles (three.js, `orbit.ts`) on a pencilled, tilted ring seen in perspective: they swell coming round the front and fade behind, hurry through the near side, spin with their subject's mark inked on, shed dust and cast hatched shadows on the floor. Each tows a tailor's tag with its question ("Why economics?"); pulling it, or clicking the ball, floats up a small window with the answer, tied to its ball. The questions and answers are in `answers.ts`; the avatar is cut out of `design/education/education.png` by `npm run education:art`. With reduced motion the balls hold still.
- **Studio** (`/studio`): owner-only. Write, import `.md` files, draft, publish and delete. Only one GitHub account can sign in.
- **Projects sketchbook** (`app/components/projects/`, `content/projects/`): a spiral-bound pad with one project per page, each laid out as a drafting sheet (a title block, the drawing, numbered points and a spec table); each logo draws itself in pencil, then the details are written in. See [docs/projects.md](docs/projects.md).
- **Contact** (`app/components/contact/`, `app/api/contact/`): a transmission pipeline scene where the tailor weaves the visitor's words into a packet, the coder breaks the errors blocking the route, and the dakpion runs it to the post box, all while it really sends; messages land in an owner-only studio inbox. See [docs/contact.md](docs/contact.md).
- **Chapter seams** (`app/components/site/ChapterSeam.tsx`): the scroll-drawn thread that carries the reader from one home-page section to the next. Each chapter is numbered like a pattern piece (01–04, the same numbers as the masthead's nav), with a tape-measure rule that is measured out as the thread is drawn and a line in the reading face.
- **The desk** (`app/globals.css`): the paper everything sits on, shaded in burgundy pencil down both edges, with a few stray marks left on the bare paper between the cards: pencil (a quick swipe of shading, a pencil tried out, a slipped line), on two tiles of different sizes so they never repeat in step; and a drop of burgundy watercolour in every gap between sections (a mottled wash with the dark edge paint leaves as it dries, and the droplets it threw off as it fell), placed by the chapter seams on the side away from their labels. All drawn by `npm run desk:strays`.
