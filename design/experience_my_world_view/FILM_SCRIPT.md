# ALL OF US: the anime cut (shot list)

A film of 90 seconds that plays by itself on the world view page. Same story,
same two recordings, same timings as before. What changed is how it is
filmed: about forty short shots with hard cuts, close-ups, speed lines and a
hold before every explosion, instead of one flat wide shot.
Everything is drawn in code: no pictures.

## The look

- **Style:** punk-revolution anime. Angular shapes, flat colour in two tones (cel shading), brush lines that taper, huge words, speed lines and focus lines.
- **Colour tells the story:** the factory is cold blue-grey; the revolt is a hot sunset (red, orange, magenta); the ending is golden dawn. Red is the people, gold is money and then light. No black screens.
- **People:** simple figures with anime proportions, a hair silhouette and clothes that whip in the wind. No faces, except eyes in close-up.
- **The mark:** the strip torn from the belt is tied round the head as a headband. Everyone who stands ties one.
- **Motion:** figures move on twos (12 poses a second); camera and effects run smooth. Big moments go: anticipation, hold, release.
- **Type:** the same few words, slammed in: `14 HOURS`, `PAY: 2`, **NO.**, **TWO.**, **TEN.**, **ALL OF US.**, **NOT A LADDER. A TABLE.**
- **Sound:** the two recordings and effects. No music in the factory.

## Around the film

- **Start:** title card, **Play with sound** / *play in silence*.
- **Timeline:** a mark for each of the eight scenes; space pauses; arrows skip.
- **Explore:** loops the scene. In the line shot, click a worker: their eye lights and the headband goes on. During the squeeze, press and hold: the press can't come down. Anywhere: drag to tear the picture. Any frame saves as a poster.
- **Reduced motion:** eight still posters with captions. **No JavaScript:** the film in words.
